# Release history

## Unreleased · Surveys

- The pulse of HR communication becomes its own, more professional app, **Surveys** (page `encuestas`). Existing
  surveys and answers are kept (series `PULSO`); the HR communication tab now leads to the new app.
- **Prepare** (HR): **templates** (monthly pulse, annual engagement, wellbeing, remote work and flexibility, onboarding
  or blank), **eight question types** (agreement scale, 1 to 5 scale, eNPS, yes/no, single choice, multiple choice,
  statement matrix and text), required or optional questions, **sections** and ten **dimensions**; **draft**,
  **preview**, opening **now or scheduled**, **duplicate**, **reminder** to those who have not answered (once a day at
  most) and closing. Up to 30 questions.
- **Answer**: step by step by sections, with a progress bar, a draft kept in the browser and a final screen explaining
  what happens with the answers.
- **Report**: participation (total and by department), **favourability index**, eNPS, **dimensions** with strengths and
  areas to improve, **trend** against the previous survey of the same series, **heat map** by department, results of
  each question, comments with search and frequent words, an **AI summary** (if the assistant is configured) and export
  to **CSV** and **print or PDF**. A manager sees only their department.
- **Action plan**: HR and managers publish actions (dimension, department, owner, deadline and status) that everyone who
  received the survey can see, on a board by status and in the report.
- **Anonymity by design** is unchanged: answers without user or time, a minimum number of answers to see any result and
  protection against deduction by subtraction.
- **AI**: new **`encuestas`** area with `pulso_pendientes`, `pulso_resultados` (now with favourability, dimensions and
  trend) and the new `encuestas_plan_accion` (now **43 tools**, about 7,800 tokens; `readOnly: true` publishes 29). The
  `pulso_*` tools leave the `comunicacion` area.
- Flyway migration **`V6__encuestas.sql`** (new columns in the pulse tables and the `ENCUESTA_ACCION` table) and demo
  data: this year's and last year's engagement surveys, three monthly pulses, a draft, a scheduled survey and an action
  plan. Tested on PostgreSQL 17 with 70 automatic checks and in the browser.

## Unreleased · My documents

- A ninth app, **My documents** (page `documentos`, after People), with each person's documentation:
  - **Payslips with their amounts** (gross, income tax, social security, other deductions and net): the latest one,
    compared with the previous one and with the breakdown of the gross pay, and the **year in a chart** with its totals
    and the average income tax rate.
  - **Social security reports**, **tax withholding certificates**, **contracts**, **certificates** and other
    documents, grouped by year and filtered by type, with a **PDF viewer** and download. New ones are marked until opened.
  - **Receipt**: the publisher can ask for it (contracts, by default); the person signs it in the viewer and the
    publisher is notified. A signed document can no longer be withdrawn.
  - **Send to HR**: doctor's notes, qualifications, bank details and personal documents, which HR **reviews or
    returns** with a reason («Received from staff» inbox).
- **Publish** (HR and Accounting): one by one, with the payslip amounts and the net calculated, or **in a batch**: each
  PDF is assigned to its person by the file name (DX user or full name) and the payslip amounts come in a CSV
  (`usuario;bruto;irpf;ss;otras`). The staff list shows each person's latest payslip and what is not opened or signed.
- **Privacy**: each person sees only their own; HR and Accounting, the company documents; what the staff send, only
  HR. **The manager sees nothing of their team.** Only PDF, JPG and PNG are accepted (checked by their content), up to
  6 MB.
- **My intranet** shows the new payslip and the documents to sign.
- **AI**: new **`documentos`** area with **3 query tools** (now **42**, about 7,500 tokens; `readOnly: true` publishes
  28): `mis_documentos`, `mis_nominas` («how much was I paid in October?») and `documentos_por_revisar` (HR). Signing,
  publishing, sending or reviewing documents stays in the app.
- Flyway migration **`V5__documentos.sql`** (`DOCUMENTO` and `NOMINA` tables) and demo data: PDF payslips of the last
  10 months with the extra payment, social security reports, tax certificates, a contract to sign and documents sent to
  HR in the three states. The PDFs are generated by ÓRBITA and say they are demo documents with no validity. Tested on
  PostgreSQL 17 with 60 automatic checks and in the browser (with real files and a batch with a CSV).

## Unreleased · People

- An eighth app, **People** (page `personas`, after HR communication), with three tabs:
  - **Directory**: search by name, job title, department, office, skill or language (accents and case ignored), with
    filters by department and by **availability today**. The ring around the avatar shows whether each person is in
    the office, working remotely, on a break, not available or out (with the date they are back).
  - **Org chart**: the tree of managers (`EMPLEADO.RESPONSABLE`), collapsible, with search; it opens on the branch of
    the person looking.
  - **Recognition**: public thanks between colleagues with a reason (teamwork, customer first, great idea, always
    helpful, goal achieved); the person and their manager are notified, and the sender (or HR) can remove it.
- **Profile** of each person: contact (email, phone, extension, office and location), length of service, reporting
  line, team, peers, profile (about me, skills, languages, birthday if they make it visible) and the recognition
  received. Everyone **edits their own profile**; **HR adds** the record of a DX user who does not have one yet and
  changes the **organisation details** (job title, department, manager, role, office, holiday days), with no loops in
  the org chart and HR unable to change its own role.
- **Privacy**: the directory never shows the reason for a break or the type of an absence (sick leave shows as «out»),
  nor clocking times, PIN or card; pending absences are not published and a birthday only appears if the person makes
  it visible.
- **Celebrations** of the next 30 days (visible birthdays, work anniversaries and new joiners) in the directory, and a
  **People** card in My intranet with the latest recognition received.
- **AI**: new **`personas`** area with **6 tools** (now **39**, about 6,900 tokens; `readOnly: true` publishes 25):
  `persona_buscar`, `persona_ficha`, `organigrama`, `celebraciones`, `reconocimientos` and `reconocimiento_enviar`.
  Editing the profile and the HR details stay in the app.
- **Own icons for each app** with the ÓRBITA brand (the square with the blue gradient, the orbit, the satellite and
  an own white drawing) in the header, the My intranet shortcuts and the notifications; the small icons of buttons and
  lists are still the IBM Carbon ones DX uses.
- Flyway migration **`V4__personas.sql`** (contact and profile columns in `EMPLEADO` and the `RECONOCIMIENTO` table)
  and demo data; restoring the demo also empties the recognition. Tested on PostgreSQL 17 with 91 automatic checks and
  in the browser (desktop, mobile, Spanish and English).

## Unreleased · HR communication

  to whoever is missing; private **Questions to HR** (sent, in progress, resolved; the employee rates and can reopen)

- **AI**: new area **`comunicacion`** with **7 tools** (now **33 tools**, about 5,800 tokens; `readOnly: true` publishes
  20). `solicitud_comentarios` and `solicitud_comentar` also take questions to HR (`clase` = `CONSULTA`). Acknowledging

- Flyway migration **`V3__comunicacion.sql`** (8 new tables) and demo data for the new app; restoring the demo also
  empties them. Tested on PostgreSQL 17 with 66 automated checks and in the browser.
- **HCL DX Compose look** in all seven apps, with the values measured in its Dashboard, Content Composer and
  Reporting: Inter (the font DX loads), black text at 87 % and 60 %, blue `#0550dc`, thin grey borders and no shadows,

## 3.1.2 · 2026-11-08 · DX with OpenID Connect

- ÓRBITA works when DX users sign in through **OpenID Connect** (Keycloak, Entra ID...): with the OIDC client of
  Liberty the DX session cookie gets a generated name (`WAS_n...`) instead of `LtpaToken2`. `dx.sessionCookie` now takes
  several names and `*` prefixes, by default `LtpaToken2,WAS_n*`. Tested with DX Compose CF239 and Keycloak 26.8.
- New chart value **`dx.oidcSupport`** (default `true`); `false` restores the behaviour of 3.1.1 (only `LtpaToken2` and the
  REST login). `dx.sessionCookie` is now empty by default (it follows `dx.oidcSupport`). To go back to the previous
  release: `helm upgrade orbita ... --version 3.1.1 --reuse-values`.
- **Why:** with the OIDC client feature, Liberty names the SSO cookie `WAS_n<hash>` and it cannot be changed (Open Liberty
  issue 18803); ÓRBITA answered "no DX session" to users who signed in through Keycloak.
- The theme step of the installation also works then: it signs in with the portal login form when the DX REST
  login answers without a session cookie.

## 3.0.0 · 2026-11-06 · Its own assistant, in English and Spanish

- **ÓRBITA's own AI assistant** (default): the «✨ Ask ÓRBITA» button talks to the model through **LiteLLM** with only
  the ÓRBITA tools, as the user of the DX session. A much smaller prompt than the HCL DX IQ chat, so each question uses
  far fewer tokens. Model configurable (`assistant.model`, by default the same alias as HCL DX IQ,
  `iq-general-purpose`). The HCL DX IQ integration is still available (`assistant.backend: iq`) and is meant for DX
  practitioners.
- **English and Spanish**: interface, messages, dates, amounts and notifications in the language of each user's DX
  profile (`preferredLanguage`), then the page language and the browser. Notifications are stored with their data and
  shown in the reader's language. The demo data stay in Spanish.
- **Settings of each application on its page**: in the DX edit mode, «⚙ Settings of this application» chooses the
  header parts, the footer and the language for that instance only. They are kept on upgrades.
- Upgrades keep the existing pages and their layout. Amounts can be typed as 12,30 or 12.30.
- **From 2.x**: the own assistant needs a LiteLLM; without one, use `assistant.backend: iq` or `none`.

## 2.0.0 · 2026-11-06 · Helm chart

**Installation**
- One Helm chart, **`hcl-dx-orbita`** (`oci://ghcr.io/europesip/charts`), instead of the `k8s/01`–`06` scripts. A Job
  creates the database, a Deployment runs the API, and the subchart `hcl-dx-scriptapp` 0.2.0 pushes the six Script
  Applications and creates the pages. No `kubectl cp`, no volume for the code, no `envsubst`.
- Container images built and tested by CI: **`orbita-api`** (Open Liberty 26.0.0.9, Java 21) and
  **`orbita-scriptapps`** (the built apps), on `ghcr.io/europesip/orbita`.
- **Requires PostgreSQL** (with a superuser Secret, so that the chart creates its database and user). Other databases
  will be supported in later releases.

**Users and security**
- **Who the user is, DX says**: the API forwards the user's DX session cookie to the DX profile API. The LTPA keys
  are no longer copied from the DX Web Engine, the LDAP and basic registry are no longer replicated, and the API
  stores no DX administrator or LDAP bind credentials.
- **The MCP server acts as the IQ chat user**, identified by the DX session that the Integrator forwards. The
  `usuario` argument is gone: the model can no longer choose on whose behalf it acts.
- **Demo users by role**: the demo story is written by roles and each role is played by an existing DX user
  (`demo.people`; default: the labs users `patricia`, `andres`, `macarena`, `eduardo`, `enrique`, `jordi`, `german`).
  Users are no longer added to the OpenLDAP.
- Kiosk key generated by the chart (Secret), instead of a fixed key in the code.

**Data**
- **Flyway migrations** (`V1__esquema.sql`, …) create and upgrade the schema; an upgrade never drops data. Before,
  a schema change dropped every table and reloaded the demo.
- PostgreSQL only (Derby, from the WebSphere Portal times, is gone). Database `orbita`, user `orbita`.
- Restoring the demo empties the tables (`TRUNCATE`) and loads the data again.

**Code and API**
- Repository restructured: `api/` (Maven, Java 21, Jakarta EE 10: `core`, `rest`, `mcp`), `web/`, `images/`, `chart/`,
  `docs/`. Package `com.europesip.orbita`.
- **REST API `/orbita-api/v1`** with real HTTP methods, routes and status codes (401, 422, 404, 405), described in
  `api/rutas.json` and shared by server and interface. Before: `POST /suite-api/api` with `op=…`, always HTTP 200.
- Leftovers of WebSphere Portal 8.5 removed: `portlet/java/com/demo/suite`, `web.xml`, `ibm-web-bnd.xml`, Java 8.
- Portal pages and kiosk key come from the API (`GET /v1/config`), not from the HTML of the Script Applications.
