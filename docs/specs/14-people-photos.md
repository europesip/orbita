# Photos in People, also in the DX portal header

Issue: #14 · Status: Implemented (for review) · Release: 3.2.0

## Summary
Optional photo of each person in People, shown in every ÓRBITA app and in the DX portal header (example theme), so that
ÓRBITA's directory is integrated with DX without depending on HCL People Service. Each person manages their own photo;
HR can manage anyone's. Related to #4 (People).

## Current behaviour
`Avatar` (`web/src/ui.jsx`) draws a coloured circle with the initials; `EMPLEADO` has no photo. The example theme
(`chart/hcl-dx-orbita/files/tema.mjs`) changes the Portal 8.5 header (logos, assistant button) but does not show the
signed-in user. HCL People Service, a sub-chart of `hcl-dx-deployment` 2.49.0, was evaluated on the lab cluster: its
image is not available with our `hclcr.io` account (401 Unauthorized), and depending on it would force every ÓRBITA
installation to install it.

## Specification
**Who**
- The person adds, changes or removes their own photo from their profile in People (camera button on the photo).
- HR (`RRHH`) can do it for anyone. Nobody else, managers included.
- Anyone with an ÓRBITA record sees the photos (same as the directory). Without a DX session or without a record: 401
  or 403.

**The image**
- JPG or PNG, checked by its content (not by the name or the declared type); at most 6 MB and 6,000 px per side, at
  least 32 px per side. Anything else is refused with a message (translated to English).
- The browser crops it to a central square of up to 512 px before sending it, so phone photos upload quickly.
- The server reads it, crops the central square, draws it again at **256 × 256 px** on white (for transparent PNGs)
  and stores it as **JPEG** (quality 0.86, about 5-20 KB). Nothing of the original file is kept: no EXIF (camera,
  location), no comments or hidden chunks.
- Table `EMPLEADO_FOTO` (`V7__fotos.sql`): `USUARIO` (key), `FOTO`, `TIPO`, `ACTUALIZADA` (the version) and
  `SUBIDA_POR`. Apart from `EMPLEADO`, so that the employee queries do not load images. Emptied and reloaded by "Restore
  demo data".

**API**
- `GET fotos` (op `fotos.lista`): `{"fotos": {usuario: version}}` of the people who have one.
- `PUT personas/{usuario}/foto` (op `fotos.subir`) with `{"datos": "<base64 or data: URL>"}` → `{usuario, foto: version,
  mensaje}`.
- `DELETE personas/{usuario}/foto` (op `fotos.borrar`) → `{usuario, foto: null, mensaje}`. Idempotent.
- `GET fotos/{usuario}` (own route of orbita-rest, not JSON): the image, or 404. `yo` = the signed-in user. With
  `?v=<version>` it is cached for a year (`private, immutable`); without it, `no-cache` with an `ETag` (304 when
  unchanged).

**Where it is shown**
- The interface asks for the version map once per page (shared by every app on it) and draws
  `fotos/{usuario}?v=<version>`; when a photo changes, every avatar of that person is updated at once. If the image
  fails, the initials are shown.
- People (directory, profile, reporting line, team, peers, org chart, celebrations, recognition), the header of the
  apps, My intranet, Approvals, Time off, Time tracking, HR communication, My documents and Surveys: wherever the person
  comes with their user id. Places that only have a name keep the initials; the time clock kiosk (no session) too.
- **Portal header** (example theme, `theme.userPhoto: true` by default): the photo, or the initials, and the first
  name of the signed-in user on the right of the header, after the assistant button, linked to
  `<pages.base>personas#persona=<usuario>`. A script in the theme asks `GET /yo` and `GET fotos/yo`; without an ÓRBITA
  record it shows nothing. On narrow screens only the photo.

**Demo data**: illustrated photos (drawn silhouettes generated by code, not real people) for seven of the nine demo
people; Lucía (`ventas-1`) and Elena (`tecnologia-2`) have none, so that the initials are also seen.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Given an employee, when they upload a JPG or PNG from their profile, then it appears in People, in the app header
      and in the portal header, and they can remove it.
- [x] Given an employee or a manager, when they try to change someone else's photo, then it is refused; given HR, it is
      allowed.
- [x] Given a photo with camera and location data, when it is saved, then the stored image is a 256 px JPEG without
      that data.
- [x] Given a PDF, GIF, SVG, a corrupt image, one over 6 MB or 6,000 px, or under 32 px, then it is refused with a clear
      message.
- [x] Given a DX user without an ÓRBITA record (or without a session), then they cannot read photos.
- [ ] Installs with its Helm chart on the lab cluster and the demo users can use it (pending new images: tested through
      the dev volume).

## Out of scope
Importing photos from the LDAP or Active Directory (`jpegPhoto`, `thumbnailPhoto`); photo moderation; HCL People
Service (its sub-chart is documented in the labs repository as an optional Lab 5).

## Plan
- API: `V7__fotos.sql`, `Fotos` (ops `fotos.*`, `normalizar()` with `javax.imageio`, dimensions checked before
  decoding), `Despachador.foto()` and the `fotos/{usuario}` route in `Recursos`; `Mensajes` (English); `Db.TABLAS`;
  `DemoPersonas.ilustracion()`.
- Web: `useFotos`, `fijarFoto`, `urlFoto` and `Avatar usuario=` in `ui.jsx`; `CambiarFoto` in `Personas.jsx`; `usuario`
  passed to the avatars of the other apps; `en.js`; `suite.css`.
- Chart: `theme.userPhoto`, `personasUrl` and `apiUrl` in `tema.json`; the header in `tema.mjs`.
- Risk: the server image library (`java.desktop`) must be present in the Open Liberty image (it is in the full JRE).
